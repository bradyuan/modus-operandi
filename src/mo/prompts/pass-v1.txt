MO pass {k} of {n}.

INITIAL — what is being said. Keep its meaning and facts.
CURRENT — the latest version.
EXAMPLES — writing whose style to match.

Rewrite CURRENT so it reads like the EXAMPLES. Keep the meaning and facts of INITIAL;
don't add new ones.

Send the full rewritten text with mo_output_pass(run="{run}", pass_number={k}, version=…).
If CURRENT already reads like the EXAMPLES, send mo_output_pass(run="{run}", pass_number={k}, no_change_reason=…) with a one-line reason.
