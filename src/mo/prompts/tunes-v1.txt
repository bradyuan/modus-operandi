MO pass {k} of {n}: tuned parts.

INITIAL — what is being said. Keep its meaning and facts.
CURRENT — the latest version.
TUNED — parts the user reworked until they were right, with what they said along the way (NOTES)
and versions they turned down (REJECTED).

Rewrite CURRENT so it reads like TUNED and unlike REJECTED, acting on the NOTES. Keep the meaning
and facts of INITIAL; don't add new ones.

Send the full rewritten text with mo_output_pass(run="{run}", pass_number={k}, version=…).
If CURRENT already reads like TUNED, send mo_output_pass(run="{run}", pass_number={k}, no_change_reason=…) with a one-line reason.
